## 1. Overview
Bajo Sospecha ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of our players. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle user data when you play our online multiplayer game.

## 2. Information We Collect
Because "Bajo Sospecha" is a multiplayer game operating via secure WebSocket protocols (`wss://`), we collect minimal data required to maintain active game sessions and player authentication:

- **Game Profile & Account Info:** Username, player avatars, and optional dual-currency account balances (e.g., in-game credits, Executive Pass status).
- **Technical & Connection Data:** Temporary IP addresses, device identifiers, and connection logs used solely for room matchmaking and WebSocket connection stability.

We **do not** collect personal real-world identities, precise geolocation, financial credit card information, or contact lists.

## 3. How We Use Information
The minimal data collected is used strictly to:
- Establish and maintain secure multiplayer game sessions via Render backend infrastructure.
- Save and restore in-game progress and cosmetic/Executive Pass items.
- Maintain game integrity and prevent cheating or unauthorized behavior.

## 4. Monetization & Purchases
"Bajo Sospecha" operates under a strictly fair cosmetics/Executive Pass model without pay-to-win mechanics. All financial transactions are processed securely through Google Play In-App Billing. We do not store or process payment data on our servers.

## 5. Data Sharing and Third Parties
We do not sell, rent, or share player data with third parties for marketing purposes. Data processing is limited to infrastructure providers essential for hosting multiplayer sessions:
- **Render:** Cloud hosting provider for server-side WebSocket connections.
- **Google Play Services:** Authentication and transaction processing.

## 6. Security
We utilize secure Transport Layer Security (WSS/HTTPS) encryption for all server communications to prevent unauthorized data access or interception.

## 7. Data Retention & Deletion
Player profile data is retained only as long as necessary to provide game services. Players may request account or profile data deletion at any time by contacting us at the provided email address.

## 8. Children's Privacy
"Bajo Sospecha" does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted directly on this page with an updated effective date.
